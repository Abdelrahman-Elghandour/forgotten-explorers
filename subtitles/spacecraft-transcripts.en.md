## Opportunity (`opp`)

I am Opportunity. I was designed to live for only 90 days, yet I roamed Meridiani Planum for 14 years. In 2018, a massive dust storm covered me and blocked the Sun from my panels, so I went quietly to sleep. My last message was: 'My battery is low and it’s getting dark.'

## Spirit (`spi`)

I am Spirit, Opportunity's twin. My wheels got trapped in soft sand near Troy crater. They tried to rescue me for months, then the cold winter arrived and there wasn't enough power to keep me warm. I still remain as a stationary outpost reporting what I see.

## Viking 1 & 2 (`vik`)

We are Viking 1 and 2. In 1976, we landed on Mars and sent back the first color photos from its surface. We tested the soil for microbes, yielding enigmatic results that scientists still debate today.

## InSight (`ins`)

I am InSight. I never moved, because I was placed here to listen to the heartbeat of Mars. I recorded hundreds of marsquakes, revealing the size of the planet's core. Dust gradually covered my panels, so I powered down my instruments one by one.

## Pathfinder & Sojourner (`pat`)

I am Pathfinder, carrying my little microwave-sized friend Sojourner inside. We landed using airbags and explored Ares Vallis. In 1997, I fell silent after my battery ran out.

## Apollo 11 'Eagle' (`eag`)

I am the descent stage of the Apollo 11 'Eagle' lander. On the Sea of Tranquility, two humans stood on another world for the very first time. When the journey ended, they left me behind to guard footprints that no wind will ever erase.

## Surveyor 3 (`sur`)

I am Surveyor 3. I landed two years before humans arrived, and later the Apollo 12 astronauts visited me and took my camera back to Earth for study. I am humanity's oldest friend on the Moon.

## Lunokhod 1 (`lun`)

I am Lunokhod 1, the first rover to roam another world controlled from Earth. My drivers were engineers in the Soviet Union. I kept moving for 11 months until my heating fuel ran out.

## Mariner & Venus (`ven`)

We are Mariner 2 and 10. We flew past Venus and revealed its scorching temperatures. Beneath the clouds lies not a lush oasis, but a massive furnace. After us, Soviet Venera probes landed and survived for only a few minutes.

## Huygens on Titan (`huy`)

I am Huygens, a European probe carried by NASA's Cassini spacecraft. I parachuted for two and a half hours through thick orange haze onto Titan. After landing, I transmitted for about 72 minutes from the surface until Cassini dipped below the horizon, cutting off contact and leaving me silent in the extreme cold.

## Voyager 1 & 2 (`voy`)

We are Voyager 1 and 2. Launched in 1977, we carry a Golden Record filled with sounds and images from Earth. We are now traveling through interstellar space, farther than anything human-made. We still whisper back to Earth as much as our power allows—and on November 18, 2026, Voyager 1 reaches one full light-day away, meaning its signal takes 24 hours to reach you.

## Kepler Telescope (`kep`)

I am Kepler. I stared at countless stars searching for faint dips in brightness when a planet crossed in front of them. I discovered thousands of exoplanets, and when my fuel ran out, I was retired in my orbit around the Sun.
