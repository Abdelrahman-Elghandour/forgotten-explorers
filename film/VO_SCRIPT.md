# Voice-over script for the 30-second cut

Read at a calm documentary pace (about 140 words per minute). The whole script is 74 words, which leaves breathing room in every beat. The music sits 6 dB below a normal voice, so no ducking is needed.

| In | Out | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0.6 | 4.4 | Three machines finished their missions on Mars and the Moon. Almost nobody heard how. |
| 4.8 | 8.6 | This is Forgotten Explorers: a free bilingual exhibit that lets you meet them. |
| 9.0 | 12.2 | Pick a world, land on the surface, and open the craft itself. |
| 12.6 | 15.8 | Every fact is on screen — the launch, the last contact, the diary — and a guide reads it to you. |
| 16.2 | 19.8 | A blueprint and a dust-and-power simulator show how the machine actually worked. |
| 20.4 | 23.6 | All of it is built on NASA's open imagery and mission records. |
| 24.0 | 27.8 | Twenty years of silence, told in Arabic and English. Every mission deserves to be heard. |

## Recording notes

- Speak 2–3 cm off-axis from the microphone to avoid plosives on "Pick" and "prototype".
- Leave 0.4 s of room tone before the first line and after the last; the film's own fade covers the tail.
- If a line runs long, shorten the middle sentence of that beat — never the first or last.
- Mix the voice to about −16 LUFS integrated; the music bed is already at −20.5 LUFS, so a straight sum lands close to a finished mix.
